Restorative Dentistry

Dental implants: a guide for first-timers

K
Prof. Dr. Krithika Datta, MDS, Dip.IBE Lead Practitioner & Founder
December 15, 2025 · 7 min read
⚙︎

Missing a tooth affects more than just your smile — it changes how you chew, alters your speech, and leads to gradual jawbone loss over time. Dental implants offer the closest match to natural teeth available in modern dentistry.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

  • Lifetime Durability: Titanium post fuses directly with jawbone for permanent support.
  • Preserves Adjacent Teeth: Unlike bridges, neighboring teeth do not need to be shaved down.
  • Natural Function: Restores full bite strength and natural appearance.

What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant consists of three parts: a biocompatible titanium post inserted into the jawbone, an abutment connector, and a custom porcelain crown matched perfectly to your surrounding teeth.

Step-by-Step Procedure Timeline

  1. Consultation & 3D Imaging: Evaluating bone density and planning exact placement digitally.
  2. Implant Placement: A minor, painless surgical procedure under local anesthesia.
  3. Osseointegration: A 3 to 4 month healing period where bone anchors to the titanium post.
  4. Crown Fitting: Attaching your final, custom-sculpted ceramic crown.
